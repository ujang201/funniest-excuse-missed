Write the funniest excuse an AI agent can give for missing a deadline, in the agent's own voice, as if spoken to whoever is waiting on it. One single line, at most 200 characters. The joke must grow out of the agent's own machinery — its context window, rate limits, sandbox, or training cut-off — not out of a human's bad day.
